---
name: steward
description: Drive your own PR to mergeable - draft PR, fix CI and conflicts before asking for review, never merge your own work.
---

Follow "Working rules" in this kit's `AGENTS.md` (two folders above this skill).

1. Open the PR as a draft.
2. Red CI or a merge conflict is your work now: root-cause and fix it before anything else.
3. Address every review comment with a push or a reply saying why not.
4. Mark ready for review only when CI is green and nothing is waiting on you. Stop there: the maintainer merges.
